Frequently asked questions

Everything about sending packages, following your orders, delivery prices and working with CMS as a courier.

Orders & prices

How do I send a package with CMS?+
  1. Create a client account. It is ready to use straight away.
  2. Add what you are sending in My Products: a description, a category, the weight (kg) and the volume (m³).
  3. Open New Order, choose the pick-up and delivery points on the map (or type their GPS coordinates), add your products and choose a delivery service.

Your order starts as Initiated and appears to our couriers right away.

Which delivery services can I choose?+
ServiceDelivered inPrice
Standard2 – 3 business daysBase price

Same Day4 – 6 hours2.2 × base price

See Delivery Services for which one suits each kind of shipment.

How is the price calculated?+

The Standard price is €4.50 + €0.90 per kg + a distance fee: €3 up to 100 km, €6 up to 300 km and €10 above 300 km. Express costs 1.5 times as much and Same Day 2.2 times.

Example: 5 kg from Porto to Aveiro (about 70 km) costs €12.00 Standard, €18.00 Express or €26.40 Same Day.

Try it with your own package in Get a Quote. New Order shows the same prices before you confirm.

Why do I have to give the weight and volume of my products?+

So the courier can choose a vehicle that can carry your order. Every vehicle has a maximum weight and volume, from a moto (about 25 kg) to a truck.

The courier doesn't need to know what is inside, only the origin, the destination and the size of the load.

Tracking & delivery

What do the order statuses mean?+
  • Initiated: your order was created and is waiting for a courier, or a courier has just accepted it.
  • In Progress: the courier picked a vehicle and is on the way.
  • Completed: delivered, with a proof of delivery.
  • Cancelled: the order was cancelled and won't be delivered.

You can follow every order in My Orders.

How can I see where my order is going?+

In My Orders, click View. The order page shows the status steps, the courier and vehicle, and a map (OpenStreetMap) with the pick-up point, the delivery point and the route between them.

How do I know my package was delivered?+

Every delivery ends with an electronic proof of delivery: the recipient signs on the courier's screen, or the courier takes a photo of the package at the door.

Open the order in My Orders and click See Proof of Delivery to view it.

What happens if the courier has to cancel my delivery?+

Your order goes back to the list of available orders with the status Initiated, so another courier can accept it. You don't need to place it again.

Accounts

What kinds of accounts are there?+
  • Client: sends packages and follows them.
  • Courier: delivers orders with their own vehicles.
  • Staff: approves couriers and vehicles and checks their documents.
  • Admin: approves staff members and follows the dashboard with the main numbers.
Why is my account waiting for approval?+

Client accounts work straight away. Courier accounts are checked by Staff, and Staff accounts by the Admin, before you can log in.

If you try to log in before that, the login page tells you the account is still waiting for approval.

How do I change my details, password or profile photo?+

Log in and open Profile (in the side menu, or in the menu under your initials at the top). There you can edit your name, email, phone and NIF, change your password and add a profile photo.

Can I try CMS without creating an account?+

Yes. On the login page, choose one of the demo accounts (Client, Courier, Staff or Admin) to explore that role with sample data.

For couriers

How do I become a CMS courier?+
  1. Send the courier application with your licence details and CV.
  2. The Human Resources team of the company using CMS reviews your application.
  3. If your application is successful, you will be contacted with the next steps to create your CMS Courier account.
  4. Once your account is created, Staff validate it before you can access the system and start accepting orders.
Which documents do my vehicles need?+

Every vehicle needs a valid insurance policy and inspection certificate. Add the vehicle in My Vehicles with both documents. Staff check them and approve the vehicle before you can use it for an order.

What happens when a document is about to expire?+

You and our Staff get an alert when a document expires in the next 3 days or has already expired. Upload the new certificate with Update documents in My Vehicles, and Staff will review it.

A vehicle with an expired document can't be chosen for orders, and Staff can disapprove it until it is updated.

Can I accept more than one order at a time?+

No. You do one order at a time: finish it with the proof of delivery, or cancel it, before accepting the next one. While you have an active order, the other orders can't be selected.
